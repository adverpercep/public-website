About Kirsty

Research Project Manager

Kirsty brings a decade of project management experience, driving digital marketing campaigns for global brands like Lionsgate+, NBCU, and Verizon. Joining Advertiser Perceptions in 2026, she specializes in qualitative research, helping clients reach their goals through strategic survey development, recruitment, and facilitating roundtables and interviews to ensure timely, data-driven insights.

Previously, as Director of Content Management at MediaVillage, Kirsty led the publication pipeline. She collaborated with clients and oversaw subject matter experts to expand quantitative research into 400+ articles, videos, and podcasts, uncovering deeper findings via thought leadership. Kirsty holds a B.A. in Advertising & Public Relations from Penn State.
