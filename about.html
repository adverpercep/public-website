Our History

From a two-founder idea to the industry's most trusted intelligence source.

Founded in 2002 by Ken Pearl and Randy Cohen, AP has assembled a driven team of industry professionals with hands-on experience in media, marketing, advertising, and technology. We work with all of our clients to drive their success through accurate and timely delivery of data-informed, actionable guidance.
