Frequently Asked Questions

Hub FAQ.

Who in my organization can access the Hub?

The subscription license includes unlimited seats for your company or organization — every team member who would benefit from the research can have an account.

How often are reports updated?

Frequency varies by topic. Some reports update bimonthly (high-velocity topics like programmatic and CTV), others quarterly, biannually, or annually. The forecast dashboard is refreshed quarterly with the latest macro data.

Can I export the underlying data?

Yes. Most reports ship with Excel files containing the raw data sets, including cuts by agency/marketer and large vs. mid-sized spenders.

Do you offer custom research on top of the Hub?

Yes — Hub subscribers often layer custom or syndicated work on top. See our Solutions page for more.

How do live webcasts work?

Each major report ships with a live presentation and Q&A from our Business Intelligence team. Sessions are recorded and made available on-demand. Internal guests can be invited.

What does the dashboard cover?

The proprietary US ad spending forecast spans 2019 to 2025+ with sub-forecasts for CTV, retail media, social media, spending by company size, and more.
